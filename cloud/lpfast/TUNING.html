lpfast tuning sheet

Applies to live_test.py and the live bridge (--facefix rel|abs / --facefix-preset). Live commands are typed in the terminal + Enter and take effect from the next frame. /save NAME stores everything marked saved in presets.json; /preset NAME loads it. Defaults below are the current code defaults (2026-11-05).

Expression gains (/gain <region> <value>, --gains, saved)

How much of your motion G-Star gets, per face region. 1 = your motion as is, 0.5 = half, 2 = double, 0 = off (the character's own expression), negative = mirrored. /gain all 0.8 scales every region at once.

Mode and pose (--mode, --pose, saved)

ParameterValuesWhat it does
moderel (G-Star) / abs (human refs)rel: the character's resting face + your motion (keeps his grin). abs: blends towards your actual expression shape (gain 1 = yours exactly)
poseon / offalso turn and scale his head with yours (still images; off for JoyAI output, which already moves the head)

Motion limits (/limit <region> <max | knee:max | off>, --limits, saved)

Soft caps so extreme expressions don't tear the face: motion passes unchanged up to the knee, then is compressed towards max and never exceeds it. A single value sets max (knee = 2/3 of it). Calibrated on G-Star.

LimitDefault knee:maxPrevents

brow20.05:0.08(guess, uncalibrated)
rest0.06:0.10(guess, uncalibrated)

Smoothing (/euro <min_cutoff> <beta> | off, --euro, saved)

One-Euro filter on your expression: smooths hard when you're still (webcam/low-light jitter, amplified by high gains), little when you move fast.

ParameterDefaultLowerHigher
min_cutoff (Hz)1.0calmer when still, a bit laggiermore detail, more jitter
beta5more lag on fast movessnappier on fast moves

The effect fades out completely (the plain JoyAI / source frame shows) when one of these trips.

The result panel shows the live values: w (effect strength 0..1), yaw, mouth (visibility), conf (face confidence), so you can see which gate is tripping.

Saved vs not saved. /save stores mode, pose, gains, all limits, all gates (incl. hold and visibility) and the smoothing (euro, including "off" — saved as null). Not saved (start-up flags only): --neutral-ema, --base-ema, --smooth, --source/--crop/--camera/--fps/--layout. Loading a preset first resets limits and gains to the defaults, then applies the preset, so a preset fully reproduces its settings.

Bridge: --facefix-preset NAME and live /preset NAME load the whole preset (limits, gates, smoothing) except pose, which stays off (JoyAI output already moves the head). Bridge equivalents: --facefix rel|abs, --facefix-preset NAME, --facefix-gains 'lip=..,eye=..'; live /gain, /limit, /gate, /euro, /preset.
